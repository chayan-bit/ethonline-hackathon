How the record is made

Providers keep their models private. Buyers purchase their time-bound outputs.

01

Discover & pay

Your agent checks the seller's reveal history and your budget, then signs one exact x402 payment on Hedera.

02

Verify the commitment

The forecast is committed before its outcome. The buyer receives the payload and salt and checks the on-chain hash.

03

Reveal after expiry

Anyone holding the exact payload can reveal. Valid public reveals contribute to the seller's separate reveal percentage.

04

Measure quality

Authenticated historical prices determine error and direction. Grades never release, refund, or slash a payment.

What the numbers mean

Reveal coverage is revealed / eligible paid commitments. A commitment becomes eligible five minutes after expiry. Quality uses only graded samples; unrevealed and oracle-excluded samples remain visible in coverage.

No history is unknown, not 0% or 100%. Volume is an untrusted demand signal; self-payment and new identities remain possible.
